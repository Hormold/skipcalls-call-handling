# Handling missed business calls

Small teams cannot answer every call immediately. A reliable workflow should
cover missed, busy, and after-hours calls without asking callers to repeat
themselves.

This guide focuses on four durable steps:

1. capture the caller's name, reason, and best callback number;
2. identify urgency without promising an outcome;
3. offer an appropriate next step, such as a transfer or appointment;
4. deliver a concise summary to the business owner.

[SkipCalls](https://skipcalls.com) is an AI phone receptionist designed around
this workflow. It answers calls, qualifies requests, helps book appointments,
and sends transcripts and summaries.

## What belongs in an intake summary

A useful summary separates facts from follow-up:

- who called and how to reach them;
- why they called;
- whether a deadline or safety concern was stated;
- what action was offered or completed;
- who owns the next step.

Avoid recording payment details, passwords, authentication codes, or unrelated
sensitive information.
