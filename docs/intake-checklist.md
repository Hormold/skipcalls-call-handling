# Missed-call intake checklist

Use this checklist when designing a receptionist or voicemail workflow.

## Before the call

- Define normal opening hours and after-hours coverage.
- List the requests that may be transferred immediately.
- Decide which appointment types callers may schedule.
- Prepare a fallback when transfer or booking is unavailable.

## During the call

- Introduce the business and explain how you can help.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Confirm names, phone numbers, dates, and addresses.
- Never invent availability, pricing, policy, or a completed action.
- For emergencies, direct the caller to the appropriate emergency service.

## After the call

- Send a short summary with a clear next action.
- Mark whether a transfer, appointment, or message was completed.
- Retain only the information needed for business follow-up.
- Review failed transfers and incomplete bookings as operational signals.
